Mermaid Flowchart Example

    flowchart TD

    A[Start: Scan Project Files] --> B[Parse requirements.txt / pom.xml / package.json]
    B --> C[Build Dependency Graph]
    C --> D[Detect Conflicts]

    D -->|Conflict Found| E[Trigger: dependency-check command]
    D -->|No Conflict| F[Proceed: lock environment]

    E --> G[Suggest Minimal Upgrade]
    G -->|Upgrade Specific Package| H["Trigger: package-upgrade <pkg-name>"]
    G -->|Downgrade Specific Package| I["Trigger: package-downgrade <pkg-name>"]
    G -->|Replace Package| J["Trigger: package-swap <old> <new>"]

    H --> K[Rebuild Dependency Graph]
    I --> K
    J --> K

    K --> L[Trigger: dependency-resolver --verify]
    L --> M{Conflicts Remaining?}
    M -->|Yes| G
    M -->|No| N[Trigger: lockfile-generate command]

    N --> O[End: Stable Environment Ready]
